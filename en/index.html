Academic project · 2026–2027 · In progress

Zalapp

Where do you stop when you drive a lorry? With two classmates, I researched that decision: parking, facilities, cleanliness, food, rest and up-to-date information.

My part
Coordinating the work, designing the proposal and connecting the research to the marketing.
Done
Survey, competitor analysis, SWOT with its action plan (DAFO/CAME in Spanish), segmentation and an individual marketing plan.
Status
Still in progress, with no commercial launch. The call for survey responses went out through CETM, Spain’s road haulage confederation, and other industry associations.
