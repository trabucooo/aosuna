      Academic project · 2026–2027 · In progress

      Zalapp

      Where do you stop when you drive a lorry? With two classmates, I researched that decision: parking, facilities, cleanliness, food, rest and up-to-date information.

      Also
      Segmentation and an individual marketing plan.
      Status
      Still in progress, with no commercial launch.

      Open the route logbook

      Route logbook · Zalapp

      KM 0 · 06:40

      The problem

      Illustration: a lorry facing a full lorry park, with all six spaces crossed out, and the figure 390,057.

      You pull into a stop you spotted on a generic map and there are no spaces left, the showers are out of order or the place just doesn’t feel right. Zalapp wants that doubt settled before you get there, not after.

      283,818

      heavy goods vehicles licensed for hire and reward in Spain (1 January 2026)

      Source: Spain’s Road Haulage Observatory (Observatorio del Transporte de Mercancías por Carretera)

      390,057

      secure lorry parking spaces missing across the EU

      Source: European Commission, 2025

      These are industry benchmarks, not potential users.

      KM 47 · 09:15

      The survey

      Illustration: 47 circles, one per survey response, and the figure 7.39.

      We shared it through CETM, Spain’s road haulage confederation, and other industry associations.

      47 responses

      36 employed drivers, 5 self-employed drivers, 4 fleet managers and 2 other profiles.

      7.39 out of 10: stated intention to use the app, among the 41 drivers

      Cut-off date: 16/10/2026. A non-probability, self-selected sample: it describes the people who responded, not the whole industry.

      KM 120 · 11:30

      The competition

      Illustration: a junction seen from above, with four curved exits and a diamond in the middle.

      Each exit is an alternative: press one to see where it leads.

      • An app for lorry drivers with a Premium plan at €6.99 a month or €74.99 a year.

      • General-purpose maps: they get you to the stop, but don’t tell you what it’s like.

      • Routes suited to your vehicle, not to the experience of stopping.

      • Up-to-date information, but scattered and hard to find.

      Zalapp wants to explore how good a stop is and how current that information is. It’s a positioning hypothesis, not an exclusive.

      KM 180 · 13:00

      SWOT and action plan

      Illustration: four road signs in a row, with the letters W, T, S and O.

      Press each sign to turn it over.

      Action plan (CAME in Spanish)

      • Correct Test the beta and build data with a source and a date.
      • Face Don’t promise safety or free spaces.
      • Maintain No payment alters ratings or alerts.
      • Exploit Validate with drivers and businesses before talking about partnerships.

      KM 300 · 17:45

      The beta

      Illustration: the phone showing the Zalapp beta, drawn in dots.

      • You can only rate a stop if you’re within 2 km of it.
      • Road alerts expire after 24 hours unless someone confirms them.
      • A free dashboard so businesses can reply to ratings.
      • The catalogue starts empty: we don’t copy data from Google Maps.

      Rest area · 19:30

      What we don’t promise

      Illustration: a round double-ring stamp with “Zalapp’s word” along the arc and a star in the middle.

      Zalapp’s word
      • We don’t say a place is safe.
      • We don’t guarantee free spaces.
      • No payment changes a rating, an alert or a ranking.
